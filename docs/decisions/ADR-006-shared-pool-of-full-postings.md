# ADR-006: A pool of full postings from a catalog of companies' own boards, plus pasted postings

## Status
Accepted 2026-11-13. At the phase 1 gate the owner chose a hand-curated list of boards and made Instante a personal project, so full posting text is shown and the served places follow the owner's own search. Proposed 2026-11-12 and revised after three adversarial review cycles the same day.

## Date
2026-11-12

## Context
- Earlier measurements show what works ([job-collection note](../research/job-collection.md)):
  - public ATS boards give every job in full in one call, and answer `304` with no body when nothing changed;
  - Adzuna gives 500-character snippets, too short to screen;
  - Indeed, LinkedIn, Job Bank and Workday's terms forbid automated access.
- Of 808 Canadian company boards found in an earlier survey, 468 are on Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever.
- **The legal footing, stated plainly:** the three job-board APIs are documented for employers' own careers sites. Greenhouse's GET endpoints need no authentication, and Lever tells its customers that published postings "may be scraped by third parties". None of them grants third parties a licence, and the text belongs to the employers. Lever's hosted pages carry a content signal (`use=reference`: index, excerpt and link back). Instante relies on no source forbidding its use, plus quick removal on request.
- Instante is a personal project, so the owner adds any board they want. A pasted posting covers a single job anywhere.

## Decision
- **One pool, filled on a schedule.** A person's search is a filter over the jobs visible to them, and screening runs on what passes. No user data goes to any source.
- **Sources in v1:**
  1. **The catalog:** a hand-curated list of about 50 to 100 boards on Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever, chosen for the owner's search. In the `job-pool` slice the agent proposes candidates, each checked with one request, and the owner approves the list. It lives in the repository as public board links only.

     The owner adds boards later with `sources add <link>`. Only links on `boards.greenhouse.io`, `job-boards.greenhouse.io`, `jobs.lever.co`, `jobs.eu.lever.co` or `jobs.ashbyhq.com` are accepted, each checked with one request.
  2. **Pasted postings:** title, company, link and text, private to the person and never fetched. A pasted link must be `https`, the text is capped like any posting, and a client key makes a double submit harmless.
- **Places:**
  - For on-site and hybrid jobs, structured fields are read first, such as Lever's `country` and Ashby's address. A remote job keeps "region not stated" unless its text narrows it.
  - Free text then goes through a written parser:
    - country names and ISO codes are recognized;
    - a gazetteer of Canada's provinces, territories and cities is used;
    - an ambiguity list is generated by crossing the gazetteer with a world place list above a population floor (London, Hamilton, Kingston, Victoria, Windsor, Richmond and others). A Canadian city name on its own counts as Canada unless it's on that list, which makes it unclear;
    - `City, CA` means Canada when the city is in the Canadian gazetteer and isn't a California namesake;
    - every other US state code means the US;
    - a bare "Remote" means remote with the region not stated.
- **Served places:**
  - kept: on-site and hybrid jobs in Canada, remote jobs open to Canada or worldwide, remote jobs with the region not stated, and unclear jobs;
  - dropped: jobs clearly elsewhere;
  - unclear jobs pass a person's filter only if they include them;
  - when one person's screening finds that a remote job is limited to another country, the job is filtered out for that person, with the reason and Bring back. Nobody else's view changes ([ADR-007](ADR-007-screening-one-call-verdict-in-code.md));
  - when the parser or the served places change, stored ETags are cleared, so each board is read in full once.
- **Identity:** a job is (board kind, external id). Slugs are lowercased and checked against a pattern. Lever's EU boards carry their region.
- **Reading:**
  - only `boards-api.greenhouse.io`, `api.lever.co`, `api.eu.lever.co` and `api.ashbyhq.com`;
  - manual redirects, where one off the same host is a failure;
  - paths built with `encodeURIComponent`;
  - a cap counted on decompressed bytes.

  The cadence: every 6 hours for boards with at least one job in Canada, remote-open-to-Canada, or unclear but possibly Canadian; weekly for the rest. The numbers are in the [limits table](../architecture.md#limits-and-windows).
- **Closing:**
  - two complete answers in a row without a job close it;
  - a `304` repeats the last complete answer, so a quiet board still closes the postings it dropped;
  - if one answer would close more than half of a board's open jobs (and at least 5), closing waits for the next read;
  - three `404`s in a row mark the board gone and close its open jobs;
  - other failures, partial reads and `429`s close nothing.
- **Change detection:** a hash of the normalized text (whitespace, links and dates removed). A cosmetic edit doesn't trigger screening again.
- **Text:** HTML becomes Markdown in the document service, with images stripped, and is rendered without raw HTML.
- **Retention:** closed jobs leave the pool at once; their rows go 30 days later unless a person's row references them. `job.source_id` is `RESTRICT`. People keep seeing jobs they acted on ([`architecture.md`](../architecture.md#data)).
- **Takedowns:**
  - `source_block` (by board or company) is honoured by seeding, adding and fetching;
  - `sources block` closes and purges a board's postings, except people's own Applied snapshots;
  - a takedown address goes in the footer before anyone besides the owner uses Instante;
  - "a blocked board is never fetched again" is a tested criterion.
- **Terms review:** each quarter, and before adding a source, re-read the terms, `robots.txt` and content signals of the API hosts and the hosted-board hosts.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| People follow boards themselves (paste a board link) | Later | Link parsing, a board check, private-until-shared visibility, caps and cleanup, for a need the owner can serve by hand. Trigger: more than 5 board requests a month |
| Himalayas | Later | Allowed with duties, but aggregators are about half its top results, and de-duplication needs company matching. Trigger: fewer than 5 remote Yes or Maybe jobs per person per week |
| A Common Crawl catalog builder | Later | Rate-limited, and its terms advise legal counsel before use. A curated list seeds v1. Trigger: the seed goes stale, or more boards are wanted than requests bring |
| Per-person fetching of searches | Rejected | Requests grow with people, and people's queries go to third parties |
| SerpApi Google Jobs; Active Jobs DB | Later, owner's call | They reach Workday and iCIMS employers. Paid, and the text comes through third parties |
| Adzuna | Later | Snippets only; hosted use needs a licence |
| Other ATS systems | Later | Undocumented or one call per job; low yield in earlier measurements |
| Remotive, We Work Remotely, Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Job Bank, Workday sites | Rejected | Their terms (We Work Remotely's page couldn't be read; the claim is unverified) |

## Consequences
- **Coverage leans to tech.** Onboarding shows how many open postings match, and the tracked target is at least 30 for the owner's search.
- **The legal exposure** is small while the owner is the only reader. Showing employers' text to anyone else would raise it, so Lever's excerpt-and-link signal is on the checklist for other people using Instante.
- **Board APIs** publish no rate limits and could start requiring keys. Adapters are isolated and tested against recorded answers, and `sources report` shows their health.
- **Closing lags** by up to two reads: about 12 hours on 6-hour boards, up to 2 weeks on weekly ones.

## Doubts raised
Single-model reviews; the owner chose to skip a cross-model second opinion.
- **Cycle 1:**
  - "A `304` stopped postings closing on quiet boards" (blocker): valid. A `304` repeats the last answer, and the research note is corrected.
  - "Unknown places flood the pool with US jobs; `CA` reads as California": valid. The parser spec, and unclear jobs hidden by default.
  - "Served places were hard-coded": valid. An owner question, made safe by the ETag reset.
  - "Case-variant slugs; redirects, slug injection and compressed sizes": valid. Closed in the HTTP seam.
  - "No way to carry out a takedown": valid. `source_block`.
  - "Any invitee could add any board for everyone": valid. In cycle 2 the feature left v1.
  - "Company names merge employers; the Himalayas skip loses jobs": valid. No unique names, and no Himalayas.
  - "The legal footing was overstated": valid. Restated.
  - "v1 carried a second source type, aggregator classification and a crawl builder": valid. All three moved to later.
  - "Images in postings": valid. Stripped.
  - "Whole-board closes; cosmetic re-screens": valid. The hold and the normalized hash.
- **Cycle 2:**
  - "The parser over-corrected: bare Canadian cities hidden, 'Toronto, CA' dropped, structured fields ignored, cadence rules inconsistent": valid. Fixed above, with one cadence rule.
  - "Nothing closes a board that starts answering 404": valid. Three in a row mark it gone.
  - "US-only remote jobs with no stated region get screened per person": valid. A shared move was tried first; cycle 3 reversed it (below).
  - "The fallback seed was unplanned": valid. A curated list, which the owner then chose.
  - "Followed boards are complexity v1 doesn't need": valid. Moved to later.
- **Cycle 3:**
  - "One person's screening removed jobs for everyone": valid. It's now a per-person filter.
  - "The parser misfiled 'Toronto, CA' and US namesakes, and unclear jobs slowed their boards to weekly": valid. The `City, CA` rule, country codes, a generated ambiguity list, structured fields only for on-site jobs, and possibly-Canadian jobs counted for cadence.

## Revisit when
- More than 5 board requests a month: let people follow boards.
- Fewer than 5 remote Yes or Maybe jobs per person per week: Himalayas.
- Many pasted links from one system: that system's reader.
- One pass of all boards no longer fits in 6 hours: adapt cadence per board.
- Any source's terms or signals change.
